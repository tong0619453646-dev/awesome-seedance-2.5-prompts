```text
Create a ten second cinematic live action illusion in a sixteen by nine frame at thirty frames per second. Begin inside a faithfully recreated late nineteen nineties desktop paint program. Use a cool gray window frame, navy title bar, simple menus, chunky pixel tool icons on the left, scroll bars, and small color swatches along the bottom. The canvas shows a saturated blue sky with soft white clouds and a vivid green grassy hill, nostalgic but photographed with realistic depth, subtle cloud movement, and light atmospheric haze. 0 to 1.2 seconds. Hold a locked frontal camera on the full paint window. The empty landscape fills the canvas. A slim young adult man rises smoothly from the bottom center with his back to camera. He wears a red athletic shirt with white collar and sleeve stripes, a backward royal blue cap, a thin silver chain, and black ink forearm tattoos. Give his movement real body weight, natural shoulder motion, and slight cloth drag. 1.2 to 2.5 seconds. He lifts his tattooed right arm toward the upper left of the canvas as though touching an invisible drawing tool. Keep the interface perfectly stable and sharp. He turns clockwise toward camera, lowers his hand, and settles into a relaxed waist up pose at center. Use soft frontal daylight, a gentle key from camera left, believable skin texture, clear eyes, fine facial hair, and a faint contact shadow. 2.5 to 6.2 seconds. He looks into camera with a playful calm expression, raises one index finger, and draws in midair. A warm ivory line appears exactly at the fingertip and follows every gesture without delay. It looks like a real textured paint stroke with slight thickness variation, dry brush edges, and a soft glow. He draws one continuous five point star around his body. Make the tall upper point above his cap, the right point beside his shoulder, the lower left point across his torso, the lower right point, then close the shape near his raised hand. Preserve correct hand anatomy, stable identity, perfect line continuity, and natural reactions from his free hand. 6.2 to 7.4 seconds. When the star closes, perform a smooth optical push into the canvas until the interface edges slide beyond the frame. The move should feel like a real camera passing through a monitor, with subtle lens breathing and no cut. He smiles softly and lifts both palms in a small satisfied reveal while the completed ivory star floats around him. 7.4 to 10 seconds. He glances right and walks naturally out of frame. Keep the camera locked on the empty hill and sky. The star remains suspended, slowly loses brightness, and becomes slightly transparent without vanishing. End with one cloud drifting through its center. Maintain one continuous shot, realistic motion blur, natural blinking, stable clothing and tattoos, accurate perspective, clean edges, no warped limbs, no duplicate fingers, no facial changes, no flicker, no random objects, no extra people, and no artificial plastic skin. Make it feel like a practical live action performance composited into an authentic retro computer interface. Audio. Add an original playful retro electronic instrumental at about one hundred and five beats per minute with a warm drum machine groove, soft bass, bright synth plucks, and subtle digital ambience. Sync a light mouse click to the first hand gesture, delicate paint stroke swishes to the fingertip, a sparkling chime when the star closes, and a soft whoosh during the push into the canvas. No vocals and no dialogue.
```

An elderly night janitor cleans an abandoned Art Deco planetarium during a thunderstorm. The projected moon becomes physically real and drags the entire auditorium into its gravity. [0–1.5s] Shot 1: Macro shot of mop water trembling in its bucket although the floor is still. [1.5–3s] Shot 2: Wide tracking shot follows the janitor beneath the dark planetarium dome. [25.5–27s] Shot 18: The moon implodes silently into streams of silver dust and collapsing orbital debris. [27–28.5s] Shot 19: Gravity returns; seats slam into place while the janitor lands safely in his rolling bucket. [28.5–30s] Shot 20: Quiet final close-up: he discovers a marble-sized moon in his pocket while the water in his bucket produces a tiny tide. **提示词:**

```text
1980s film, authentic live-action footage shot on 35mm with heavy grain, vibrant yet gritty saturated colors, practical neon lighting mixed with harsh strobes and fog. Fast chaotic editing packed with abrupt cuts, glass reflections, POV inserts, strobe flashes and dynamic movement to create total disorientation and panic. Real practical squib blood, rubber tentacles and physical stunts. Period-accurate 80s performances: big hair, colorful outfits, leg warmers, exaggerated fear and desperate physicality. Visualized synth atmosphere through pulsing lights and sudden dead silence.
[IMAGE REFERENCES] Generate from scratch a consistent group of five mid-80s young adults with distinctly different looks—tall blonde mullet guy in neon shirt, short chubby dark-curly guy in Hawaiian print, slim big-curled redhead girl in neon dress, athletic black-ponytailed girl in crop top, lanky glasses brunette in pastel polo—in colorful yacht-party fashion and one original giant octopus killer with thick rubber tentacles and gaping beak. All on a neon-drenched luxury yacht in pure practical 1980s slasher style.
[TIMELINE SECOND BY SECOND] 0-3s: [Rapid Disorientation Montage] Quick jarring cuts on the dimly lit yacht deck at night. Neon lights pulsing, five friends laughing and dancing with drinks, music blasting. Inserts: champagne spilling, one girl’s smile fading as mist rises from the water, glass reflection briefly showing a massive tentacle that vanishes. Strobe flicker. Laughter dies as engines are dead and no land in sight.
3-6s: [Rising Panic Montage] Fast cuts as panic spreads. Tall blonde runs to the helm, radio smashed. Close-up of broken mic. Two girls huddle near railing, eyes wide, calling names that echo. Low-angle POV gliding through thick fog. Drink glass tips. They split—one to cabins, one to stern. Water reflections show tentacles moving unseen.
6-9s: [First Attack Sequence] Sudden violence, rapid-fire cuts. Giant octopus erupts from foggy water. Quick cuts: massive tentacle lit by strobe, slamming down, bright squib blood spray across pink jacket. Body drops hard on wet deck. Others scream and scatter, slipping. One girl falls. Octopus advances with heavy tentacle movements while neon keeps pulsing.
9-12s: [Full Chaos & Practical Kills] Frenzied montage. Multiple cuts: guy racing for life raft, tentacle appears behind him. Girl hides behind bar, tentacle smashes through, practical blood on counter. Another cornered against cabin wall, lifted and slammed, body slides leaving red streak. Feet scraping, bodies falling, fog swirling around practical gore. Violent strobes. Survivor swings boat hook, dull thud on tentacle.
12-15s: [Climax & Lingering Threat] Fast cuts. Last two desperately lower a life raft. One yanked into fog and water, scream cut short. Final girl turns, face sweat-and-blood covered, backing on slippery deck. Wide shot of yacht littered with bodies under pulsing neon. Octopus rises again, tentacle cracked but massive. She runs into darkness as strobe freezes on the gaping beak. Slight film burn at end.
[STYLE & QUALITY BOOSTERS] Photorealistic 1980s film emulation, heavy natural grain and reel artifacts, authentic practical squib blood and rubber-tentacle stunts, coherent physics with realistic slips and momentum on wet wooden deck.
```

### 5. 黑白 35mm 咖啡馆文艺片

作者 [@digitalwindai](https://x.com/digitalwindai) · 30 秒 · 16:9 · **[▶ 在 APIKO 运行](https://apiko.app/zh/seedance-2-5-prompts?utm_source=github&utm_medium=case&utm_campaign=awesome-seedance-2.5-prompts&utm_content=cinematic-05#cinematic-05)** · [原帖](https://x.com/digitalwindai/status/2083281155286085911)

先用一段 STYLE 写胶片时代的词，再放一个短场景。

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d26f6d43-23f9-494b-a75e-d4aa1c2f9cd8

**提示词:**

```text
STYLE
Black-and-white 35mm arthouse cinema, 1960s European café-film look. High-contrast monochrome, soft film grain, gentle bloom on the lamps, shallow depth of field on close-ups.

LIGHT
Night interior lit only by practicals: hanging globe lamps, the espresso machine's glow, and cold light through the window from the lunar plain. Soft key from the window side, deep shadow in the corners, clear catch-lights in the eyes.

COLOR
None. Full grayscale, rich mid-grays on skin, no tint of any kind.

TEXTURE
Fine grain. Chrome holds soft rolling reflections, glass refracts the lamps in small bright points, skin keeps visible pore-level texture with no smoothing.

MOOD
Quiet, intimate, unhurried — a conversation overheard in a place that is ordinary to the people in it.

SCENE CONTEXT
A near-empty late-night café on the surface of the moon. A man and a woman sit across a small round table beside the wide window, talking about the water. Neither of them ever looks outside.

CHARACTERS

The woman: early thirties, dark bob haircut, intelligent face, dark turtleneck with the cuffs visible at her wrists, thin earrings, light coat over her chair. She sits camera-left. Identical in every shot.

The man: mid-thirties, dark slightly messy hair, light stubble, shadowed under-eyes, dark jacket over an open-collar light shirt, jacket sleeve visible at his wrists. He sits camera-right. Identical in every shot.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: their small round table. Her espresso cup and the single tall glass of water sit on her side, close to her right hand. His espresso cup and spoon sit on his side. Nothing else on the table.
Midground: small round tables, worn tiled floor, hanging globe lamps, chrome espresso machine at the counter.
Background: a wide window along the wall beside them. Beyond the glass, a grey lunar plain with low craters, black sky, and Earth small and low near the horizon.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Interior medium two-shot, 47°, eye level. The woman sits camera-left, the man camera-right, the window and lunar plain behind them, café interior deeper in the room. Her hands are around her cup with the water glass beside it, his forearms rest on the table.

FORMAT MODE
Timed multishot with HARD CUTS only at 6.0s, 8.5s, 11.0s, 13.0s, 16.0s, 19.0s, 22.5s, 25.0s and 28.0s. The camera cuts only at these points.

STORYBOARD

SHOT 1 (0.0–6.0s)
Medium two-shot, 47°, faint 1–2 cm breathing drift.
The woman, eyes on him: "Do you think people get more honest when they're tired?"
The man answers late, eyes coming up off the table: "No. Their lies just get less inventive."

SHOT 2 (6.0–8.5s)
Extreme close-up, 12°, of the woman's hands on her side of the table, dark turtleneck cuffs at her wrists. Her fingers leave her cup, lift the tall glass of water, and set it back down in the same place, the water rocking once.
The woman, speaking over the image: "It's different tonight."

SHOT 3 (8.5–11.0s)
Close portrait of the man, 18°, eyes down.
The man: "They changed the water."

SHOT 4 (11.0–13.0s)
Extreme close-up, 12°, of the man's hand on his side of the table, dark jacket sleeve at his wrist, turning his spoon a half-turn on the saucer.
The woman, speaking over the image from off-frame: "Again?"

SHOT 5 (13.0–16.0s)
Medium close-up of the man, 29°, lunar plain soft behind his shoulder, eyes still on his cup.
The man: "The old one remembered too much."

SHOT 6 (16.0–19.0s)
Close-up of the woman, 18°. Her eyes come up and hold his.
The woman: "Water can't remember people."

SHOT 7 (19.0–22.5s)
Medium close-up of the man, 29°. He swallows once, then looks up at her.
The man: "Probably not. But people can't remember the past either."

SHOT 8 (22.5–25.0s)
Medium two-shot, 47°, lunar plain behind them. She leans in slightly.
The woman: "Then what do we remember?"

SHOT 9 (25.0–28.0s)
Close portrait of the man, 18°. One breath, then his eyes come up and hold hers.
The man: "The last version we could live with."

SHOT 10 (28.0–30.0s)
Close-up of the woman, 18°, the lunar plain and black sky soft behind her through the glass. No dialogue. Her face stills, one slow blink, her eyes drop toward her cup and then drift slightly aside. The shot holds on her to the last frame, the image staying full and lit.

PERFORMANCE
Both faces stay mostly still, so small movements read. Emotion comes through eyelids, brow corners, jaw and breath rather than expression. Blinks work as punctuation — withheld through the important lines, released after. She runs from light and half-joking, to quieter, to genuinely curious, to quiet recognition at the end. He runs from dry amusement, to flat matter-of-fact, to unguarded tiredness on the last line. Living eyes with catch-lights, natural skin, precise lip sync whenever a speaking face is in frame. Every line is delivered unhurried and complete inside its own shot.

PHYSICS
Steam curls off the cups and the espresso machine. Water rocks and settles in the glass. The cup meets the saucer with weight, the spoon rings faintly on chrome. Nothing beyond the window moves.

AUDIO
The low hum of an enclosed room, a faint air-system whisper, the hiss of the espresso machine, a spoon against a saucer, a swallow of water. No music anywhere. Voices sit close and dry, as in a small room late at night.

OUTPUT SETTINGS
Black and white throughout, real-time, no slow motion.

POSITIVE LOCKS
The image stays fully monochrome in every shot.
The woman speaks her five lines and the man speaks his five lines, exactly as assigned in the storyboard, and neither ever speaks the other's words.
The single glass of water stays on the woman's side of the table for the whole scene, and only she touches it.
The woman stays camera-left and the man camera-right in every two-shot.
Hands in the extreme close-ups are identified by sleeve — turtleneck cuff for her, jacket sleeve for him.
The table holds only two espresso cups on saucers, two spoons and one glass of water.
The interior reads clearly as a café — table, cups, lamps, counter, espresso machine.
The window shows a grey lunar plain, black sky and small distant Earth wherever it is visible, and nothing beyond the glass ever moves.
Both characters keep their exact appearance and seats across every shot.
All ten lines are spoken in full and finish inside their own shot.
Cuts land only at the stated seconds.
The video ends on the held close-up of the woman's face, the image full and lit to the final frame.
```

Ultra-realistic survival thriller set in a flooded Indian village under heavy overcast skies. Brown floodwater fills narrow lanes between submerged houses, broken fences, floating debris and tin roofs barely above water. The tone is grounded, tense and cinematic, with natural panic, handheld urgency and no exaggerated monster-movie feel. The crocodile must feel heavy, real and terrifying — a large aggressive crocodile cutting fast through the muddy water.
