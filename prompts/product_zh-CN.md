### 64. 整脊诊所的社媒宣传片

作者 [@bmx_ai13](https://x.com/bmx_ai13) · 30 秒 · 16:9 · **[▶ 在 APIKO 运行](https://apiko.app/zh/seedance-2-5-prompts?utm_source=github&utm_medium=case&utm_campaign=awesome-seedance-2.5-prompts&utm_content=product-01#product-01)** · [原帖](https://x.com/bmx_ai13/status/2083501494150574415)

一段话写清空间、疗程和氛围，像商业广告脚本。

<a href="https://apiko.app/zh/seedance-2-5-prompts?utm_source=github&utm_medium=case&utm_campaign=awesome-seedance-2.5-prompts&utm_content=product-01#product-01"><img src="https://media.apiko.app/prompts/seedance-2-5-prompts/product-01.webp" width="560" alt="整脊诊所的社媒宣传片 – Seedance 2.5"></a>

**提示词:**

```text
Create a 30 second 16:9 cinematic social video set inside a modern chiropractic and mobility clinic during late morning. The room has pale warm walls, clean wood flooring, black padded treatment tables, gray visitor chairs, a few green plants, subtle wall art, and tall windows overlooking leafy trees. Use natural daylight mixed with soft ceiling light, realistic skin texture, true fabric behavior, accurate anatomy, physically believable contact, consistent faces, and natural micro expressions. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no surreal motion.

From 0 to 4 seconds, open on a wide establishing shot with a slow handheld push through the clinic. A calm male practitioner in a charcoal shirt guides an adult male client in a white shirt toward a treatment table. Other staff move softly in the distant background. The mood feels professional, relaxed, and observational.

From 4 to 8 seconds, cut to a medium seated shot. The client sits centered while the practitioner stands behind him, gently checking shoulder level and neck rotation. Use small natural camera drift, shallow depth of field, window light on one side of the face, and realistic eye contact. Show quiet anticipation without exaggerated acting.

From 8 to 12 seconds, move into a close side angle as the practitioner carefully positions the head for a controlled neck release. Keep the motion restrained and anatomically correct. Capture a quick release, a subtle facial reaction, a sharp breath, and an immediate smile. Let the camera react with a tiny authentic handheld movement rather than a dramatic shake.

From 12 to 17 seconds, cut to a medium wide side view of the client lying on his side on the table. The practitioner braces the shoulder and pelvis, pauses, then performs one compact lumbar adjustment. Show realistic weight transfer, table cushion compression, shirt folds, breathing, hand pressure, and a brief surprised laugh from the client.

From 17 to 22 seconds, cut to the client lying face down. The practitioner palpates the upper back, stacks both hands, and applies one controlled thoracic compression. Use a slightly lower camera angle, soft reflections on the table, gentle background activity, and a brief focus pull from the hands to the client’s relieved expression.

From 22 to 26 seconds, transition to a head of table view. The client lies on his back while the practitioner places a black traction strap beneath the base of the skull. Build tension slowly and safely, then deliver one short controlled pull. The client reacts with wide eyes, then laughs and covers his face for a moment.

From 26 to 30 seconds, finish with the client sitting upright, rolling his shoulders and turning his neck comfortably while the practitioner stands beside him. End on an honest shared smile and a slow pullback that reveals the bright clinic. Keep the final moment calm and satisfying, not promotional.

Audio should feel captured on location with soft room ambience, distant clinic movement, clothing rustle, table creaks, natural breathing, quiet conversation without clearly audible words, and crisp but not exaggerated adjustment sounds. Add a low volume modern instrumental track with warm piano, soft bass, light percussion, and a gentle rise at each release, ending on a clean resolved note.
```

### 65. 写实风时装变装

作者 [@bmx_ai13](https://x.com/bmx_ai13) · 30 秒 · 16:9 · **[▶ 在 APIKO 运行](https://apiko.app/zh/seedance-2-5-prompts?utm_source=github&utm_medium=case&utm_campaign=awesome-seedance-2.5-prompts&utm_content=product-02#product-02)** · [原帖](https://x.com/bmx_ai13/status/2083372393649832355)

把换装拆成相连的节拍，装进 30 秒的画面里。

<a href="https://apiko.app/zh/seedance-2-5-prompts?utm_source=github&utm_medium=case&utm_campaign=awesome-seedance-2.5-prompts&utm_content=product-02#product-02"><img src="https://media.apiko.app/prompts/seedance-2-5-prompts/product-02.webp" width="560" alt="写实风时装变装 – Seedance 2.5"></a>

**提示词:**

```text
Create a 30 second photorealistic fashion transformation video in a 16:9 landscape frame. A stylish young woman with long softly waved brown hair, warm medium skin, round black glasses, small earrings, natural makeup, and consistent facial features stands in the same modern office washroom or dressing area for the entire video. The background has large charcoal gray stone tiles, a pale ceiling, a slim black wall rail on the left, and a narrow metal partition line on the right. Keep every architectural line perfectly fixed across all cuts.

Camera is locked on a tripod at eye level, medium full shot from mid thigh upward, using a natural 35 mm equivalent smartphone lens. The subject remains centered with enough space on both sides for the landscape composition. No camera shake, zoom, reframing, or perspective change. Use crisp realistic detail, true skin texture, fine hair strands, believable fabric folds, accurate hands, subtle breathing, and tiny posture adjustments. Lighting is soft cool overhead office lighting with gentle facial fill, mild reflections on the gray tiles, consistent exposure, and realistic shadow direction.

Begin with her palm very close to the lens, briefly obscuring most of the frame. She pulls her hand away and reveals the first outfit, a sleeveless pale blue floral office dress with a light scarf over one shoulder. She looks calmly into the camera, lowers her arm, places one hand on her hip, glances upward with a mildly unimpressed expression, then folds her arms. Match this exact folded arm pose for the first invisible jump cut.

Across the next twenty four seconds, reveal nine distinct office outfits through clean pose matched cuts timed to the music. Use a lemon yellow floral kimono blouse, a relaxed ivory button shirt with navy trousers, a fitted black sleeveless top with dark tailored pants and a brown belt, a soft blush pink top with black trousers, a deep green crew neck blouse, a vivid blue paisley shift dress, a black and white dotted peplum blouse, a camel blazer over a cream top, and a final elegant burgundy wrap dress. Between changes, give her natural confident gestures such as a small shoulder bounce, two thumbs up, hands settling on hips, crossing her arms, turning her chin, smoothing a cuff, lightly flicking her hair, pointing toward her cheek, and smiling directly at camera. Each gesture should begin in one outfit and end in the next so the cut feels practical and intentional, never like body morphing.

Hold each look long enough to read clearly, roughly two seconds per outfit, with cuts landing on strong beats. Preserve identical body position, hair direction, glasses placement, limb anatomy, background geometry, and lighting at every transition. Avoid warped fingers, changing facial identity, floating clothing, cloth melting, duplicated accessories, sudden camera motion, or artificial beauty smoothing.

For the final four seconds, she lands in the burgundy dress with one hand on her hip, gives a warm playful smile, taps one finger lightly against her cheek, then reaches toward the lens until her palm fills the frame, creating a natural practical ending. Do not add captions, logos, watermarks, interface graphics, or generated text.

Music should be an upbeat polished fashion pop instrumental at about 112 beats per minute with tight percussion, light bass, finger snaps, bright synth accents, and clean beat drops for every outfit change. Add subtle realistic clothing movement, bracelet clicks, and a soft hand whoosh at the opening and closing. The finished result should feel like a real creator filmed several office looks in one location and edited them precisely to music.
```

### 66. 8 秒踩点换装

作者 [@blocktom](https://x.com/blocktom) · 8 秒 · 9:16 · **[▶ 在 APIKO 运行](https://apiko.app/zh/seedance-2-5-prompts?utm_source=github&utm_medium=case&utm_campaign=awesome-seedance-2.5-prompts&utm_content=product-03#product-03)** · [原帖](https://x.com/blocktom/status/2083361106027061344)

从图中保留脸型和布局，再让每套造型对应一个节拍。

<a href="https://apiko.app/zh/seedance-2-5-prompts?utm_source=github&utm_medium=case&utm_campaign=awesome-seedance-2.5-prompts&utm_content=product-03#product-03"><img src="https://media.apiko.app/prompts/seedance-2-5-prompts/product-03.webp" width="320" alt="8 秒踩点换装 – Seedance 2.5"></a>

> 📎 这条需要你自己上传参考图或参考视频。

**提示词:**

```text
参考上传图片中的女主形象、脸型、五官、肤色、黑长发发型、身材比例和整体画面布局，生成一段8秒、9:16竖屏的甜酷风高机位卡点换装视频。

【人物与画面】
中央女主保持上传图片中的脸型、五官、肤色、黑长发和整体甜酷气质完全一致。
初始造型使用中央穿搭：运动装（短袖运动T恤 + 高腰运动短裤/短裙 + 运动鞋）。

画面四周固定悬浮4个小型穿搭人物，保持图片中的位置、比例、服装和朝向：
左上：吊带装
右上：牛仔装
左侧中部：古风装
左下：运动装变体

四个小人物都是完整人物抠像，外沿有清楚的白色虚线描边，像悬浮贴纸。

【场景与机位】
浅灰白色无影棚，背景干净，光线柔和通透。
摄影机固定在女主前上方，采用近距离高机位俯拍（约向下30度）。
女主自然抬眼看向镜头，头部和上半身更靠近镜头。
全程固定机位，不推拉、不摇镜、不旋转、不变焦。

【换装机制与时间线】
0.00–1.80秒：女主穿运动装，身体微微前倾靠近镜头，动作轻快自然，带甜酷表情。鼠标点击左上人物，左上吊带装人物飞向中央并放大重合，完成第一次换装成吊带装，动作连续不中断。

1.80–3.60秒：女主穿吊带装，一只手轻撩头发，身体轻微侧转。鼠标点击右上人物，牛仔装人物飞向中央并放大重合，切换成牛仔装，继续原有动作节奏。

3.60–5.50秒：女主穿牛仔装，双手自然摆动或调整衣领，身体轻微下沉再起来。鼠标点击左侧中部人物，古风装人物飞向中央并放大重合，切换成古风装，动作保持连贯。

5.50–8.00秒：女主穿古风装，缓缓转回正面，衣摆自然垂落，抬眼直视镜头，以甜酷又带一点清冷的姿势结束。

【声音】
无背景音乐。保留鼠标点击声、人物飞入的短促音效、换装卡点音效，可轻微加入衣料摩擦声。

【强制约束】
- 换装前后必须保持同一张脸、同一发型、同一身材比例
- 动作方向、重心、手臂位置必须连续，不能卡顿或僵硬
- 四个描边小人物全程固定在原位置，点击后原人物不消失
- 换装必须由完整人物抠像飞入、放大、重合完成
- 不要生成矩形缩略图、人物残影、双重人物、服装叠穿、肢体畸形
- 古风服装的袖子和裙摆要有自然垂坠感，不能僵硬漂浮
- 人脸清晰不变形，皮肤质感真实，画面稳定

三、生成后微调建议
如果某一秒衣服不准，就把对应服装的描述写得更具体再重新生成。
想更丝滑可以在每个换装节点加遮挡动作（甩发、捂脸、转身）。

这个方法目前稳定性和出片效率都很高，特别适合女装电商和穿搭账号批量出内容。

需要其他风格（JK、职场、纯古风等）也可以。
```

### 67. 银色刘海与亮粉的美妆短片

按时间点标注音效，让妆容镜头对上声音提示。

<a href="https://apiko.app/zh/seedance-2-5-prompts?utm_source=github&utm_medium=case&utm_campaign=awesome-seedance-2.5-prompts&utm_content=product-04#product-04"><img src="https://media.apiko.app/prompts/seedance-2-5-prompts/product-04.webp" width="560" alt="银色刘海与亮粉的美妆短片 – Seedance 2.5"></a>

> 📎 这条需要你自己上传参考图或参考视频。

**提示词:**

```text
Scene
Polished man, early 20s, silver-white dyed comma fringe swept across forehead, subtle silver eye glitter on upper lids, small silver hoop in each ear.

Outfit
Cropped black leather jacket with silver zipper hardware over fitted white ribbed crop top, high-waisted straight-leg black leather pants, chunky black platform boots with thick rubber soles.

Space
Sleek professional dance practice room, floor-to-ceiling mirror panels on far wall, pale grey vinyl flooring with faint sheen, LED strip lighting mounted along ceiling perimeter and mirror frame edges cycling slowly cool blue ⇄ crisp white, immaculately empty ultra-modern minimalist.

Camera Open
Wide full-body at mid-chest height, 24mm equiv lens, slight low angle emphasizing boots and full silhouette.

Color Grade
High-contrast, cool cyan shadow toning, clean highlights.

Mood
Electric, controlled, precise.

Diegetic SFX Throughout
Boot strikes on vinyl, jacket leather creak on sharp arm movements, breath on exertion.

Timeline

0s — Centered ready stance, feet hip-width, arms at sides wrists cocked outward, chest lifted, eyes forward to camera.

Camera: hold static wide full-body
LED: pulse once blue→white slow wash
SFX: faint ambient room hum, vinyl floor creak under weight shift
3s — Sharp chest isolation pops forward+up x2, arms snap back simultaneously, chin lifts on each pop.

Camera: wide with slight push in
LED: cool blue intensifies at mirror edges casting twin reflections
SFX: leather jacket creak on each arm snap
6s — Weight shifts right into crisp side-step lock — right foot steps wide, left foot locks behind at heel, both arms extend hard left in opposition creating diagonal line, head tilt left.

Camera: rack to 3/4 angle showing dancer+mirror
LED: white strip brightens sharply
SFX: platform boot strike on vinyl — clean clap
9s — Double arm roll — both arms circle inward from shoulder in smooth rolling wave, hips counter-shift left, upper body undulates.

Camera: slow push to medium full-body
LED: fade briefly to dim blue creating moody strobe-adjacent flicker
SFX: audible breath exhale at wave peak
12s — Body wave travels chest→hips→knees visible full-torso ripple, arms float wide, eye contact breaks as chin tucks then lifts.

Camera: tilt slightly down to capture footwork on vinyl then return
LED: brightens back to white
SFX: rubber sole squeak on wave landing
15s — Right arm shoots forward with pointed index in sharp snap, left fist locks at hip, head snaps right, hold one beat then rapid alternating knee drops with hip emphasis, arms crossing low.

Camera: pull wide to capture footwork and full-body bounce
LED: strips cycle blue-white-blue rapidly
SFX: two sharp boot strikes on alternating knee drops
18s — Explosive two-footed jump with tucked knees, arms flung overhead, hair catching air. Landing in deep crouch with arms slamming downward palms to floor, impact absorbed through knees.

Camera: catch jump at apex then cut to low-angle looking up at landing
LED: flare to full white on landing beat
SFX: heavy dual boot impact reverberating
21s — 180° pivot on ball of left foot, right foot sweeping wide, arms open outward mid-rotation, full back shown revealing jacket back panel, then face returns chest proud.

Camera: pan follow catching mirror reflection of turn
LED: shifts to cool blue
SFX: pivot foot scrape on vinyl
24s — Deep lunge right, right hand reaches toward floor without touching, left arm sweeps fully overhead, gaze locked to camera, torso twisted open. Hold one beat then pull to standing with controlled arm ripple outward from chest along both arms in fluid wave sequence.

Camera: move to tight medium-body framing to capture arm ripple detail
LED: pulses between blue and white rapidly
SFX: leather creak on lunge-open
27s — Transition into sharp hip-isolation sequence, three crisp lateral hip pops L-R-L with upper body counter-isolating, arms frame-wide at shoulder height wrists flexed, speed increases.

Camera: hold tight medium-body
LED: holds bright white
SFX: boot heel tap on each hip accent
30s — Final freeze, snap into wide-stance hero pose, right arm punched toward camera at shoulder height, left arm cocked at shoulder fist closed, chin angled down eyes lock directly into lens beneath silver comma fringe. All motion stops.

Camera: hold medium full-body
LED: lock to full cool blue casting sharp cyan shadows across vinyl floor, mirror behind reflects frozen pose doubled
Diegetic SFX: silence drops completely; only faint room resonance remains
```
